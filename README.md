# Sample Quarterly Evaluation Report

A worked example of the quarterly evaluation report **ChiResearch Technologies, LLC** delivers to a grantee under a SAMHSA Garrett Lee Smith suicide prevention award.

Two formats, same content:

- **[Interactive version](./)** — every indicator opens to its operational definition and the delivered file each figure traces to. Switch reporting periods; filter by sub-region.
- **[PDF version](./ChiResearch_Sample_Quarterly_Evaluation_Report.pdf)** — 7 pages, the document as delivered.

## What it shows

- The five performance indicators a GLS State/Tribal award reports quarterly, with Year 1 targets and status against elapsed time
- The referral-to-service chain, where the measure most often misreported in a grant's first year sits
- Reach by sub-region, with absence flagged rather than averaged away
- Data quality and completeness stated before findings
- A real contextual baseline from the 2026 Alaska Native Injury Atlas — the Interior region's own figures and the statewide age and sex distribution, reported without ranking communities against one another
- A source register tracing every figure to a named delivered file
- The measurement framework behind all of it: counting rules, source systems, instruments, owners

## What is real and what is not

The five indicators, their definitions and the reporting cadence are taken from the published FY2026 Garrett Lee Smith State/Tribal notice of funding opportunity. External data sources are cited accurately with their real coverage and limitations.

**All program activity figures are illustrative.** They describe no real participants, no real services and no real outcomes, and they are marked as illustrative throughout.
